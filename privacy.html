Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

🛡 Our core promise: Your personal data is only kept for as long as needed to complete a trade. Once a trade is successfully completed, your personal information is permanently deleted. Only anonymised data (items traded, city, country) is retained for platform analytics.

1. Who We Are

iluX is a peer-to-peer barter marketplace that allows users to exchange goods and services without money. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Email address — to identify your account and notify you of trade activity
  • Trade listings — what you offer and what you want in return
  • Shipping address — only if you use iluX Logistics for delivery
  • City and country — for matching you with nearby traders
  • Trade activity — items exchanged, timestamps, trade status

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To match you with compatible trade partners
  • To facilitate and track the progress of your trades
  • To send you notifications about your trades
  • To moderate listings for illegal or harmful content

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Email & personal details — deleted within 30 days of a completed trade
  • Shipping address — deleted immediately after confirmed delivery
  • Trade listings — deleted after trade completion
  • Anonymised trade stats (items traded, city, country) — retained permanently for analytics. Cannot be linked back to you.
  • Beta waitlist emails — deleted within 90 days of platform launch, or immediately upon request

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Ever. We share data only with your trade partner (to complete the trade), shipping carriers (if using iluX Logistics), or authorities (if required by law).

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Withdraw consent at any time

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or deletion requests, please contact us through the iluX platform.
